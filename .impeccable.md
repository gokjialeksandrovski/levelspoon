## Design Context

### Users
Per100 is being built toward a real external user base — a nutrition/macro-
and-kcal meal-logging app, not a personal-only tool. People log meals
throughout the day, often quickly, frequently one-handed on a phone, often
right after or mid-meal.

### Brand Personality
Near-zero friction meets visual warmth. Fast enough to disappear in
seconds, but tactile and colorful enough that logging a meal feels like a
small reward rather than a chore. Delight comes from seamless
micro-interactions and inviting visuals — **never** from unsolicited pep
talks, pop-up banners, gamification badges, or feature bloat. Nutrition
tracking can trigger food anxiety for some users; tone stays quietly
encouraging without being saccharine or preachy.

### Aesthetic Direction
**"Warm Tactile Minimal"** — bridges ultra-fast developer-tool efficiency
with the comfort of a physical notebook. Supersedes the app's previous
dark-olive/chartreuse/flat-surface theme (`globals.css`, original
`Button`/`Field`/`Sheet`) — this is a deliberate full re-theme, not an
addition alongside it.

- **Theme**: both light and dark, selected by `prefers-color-scheme` (not
  a single forced default). Light base: warm cream/stone `#FDFBF7`. Dark
  base: deep espresso `#1A1816`. Never pure black/white anywhere.
- **Accents** (appetite-inspired, sparing, meaning-bearing — not
  decorative): paprika/terracotta `#E05A47`, warm amber `#E5983B`, matcha
  green `#6E9B66`, berry plum `#8B4A62`. Each maps to a specific data
  category or state; the 60/30/10 weighting rule applies.
- **Surfaces**: smooth squircles (`rounded-2xl`–`rounded-3xl`), soft
  ambient shadows, faint 1px inner borders (`border-black/5` light,
  `border-white/10` dark). Cards and inputs read as tactile physical
  tiles, not flat panels.
- **Typography**: a friendly geometric sans with slightly rounded
  terminals for UI type. Numbers (macro/kcal values, the hero content of
  this app) are bold, high-contrast, tabular; labels stay soft, muted,
  secondary. Reference point given was Plus Jakarta Sans / Satoshi /
  Outfit for the *feel* — those three are flagged reflex picks under this
  skill's own anti-monoculture rule, so the actual face gets proposed and
  confirmed against that same physical description before implementation,
  not defaulted to the first of the three.
- **Layout**: dense bento-style modular grid, high information density,
  each element living in its own self-contained interactive surface.
- **Motion**: high-stiffness/low-damping spring physics (e.g. Framer
  Motion stiffness ≈400, damping ≈28), light instantaneous
  squash-and-stretch (scale 0.97–0.98). A successful log triggers a brief
  (~150ms), low-amplitude colorful pulse/micro-particle burst — always
  non-blocking, never delaying the next action. Macro progress uses
  smooth liquid-gradient fills.
- **Haptics**: light crisp ticks on slider adjustments; a single firm
  dual-tap pop on a successful log. Speed should feel physical, not just
  look fast.

### Design Principles
1. **Speed is sacred** — every motion/haptic/feedback cue starts (or
   finishes) within ~150ms and never blocks the next action.
2. **Warmth through texture and color, not copy** — no pep-talk
   microcopy, no celebratory banners, no gamification badges.
3. **Numbers are the hero** — every macro/kcal figure gets the strongest
   typographic contrast and weight on its screen.
4. **Accent color carries meaning, not decoration** — each appetite tone
   maps to one data category/state, used sparingly.
5. **Tactile realism over flat minimalism** — surfaces read as physical
   objects (soft shadow, faint inner border, squircle geometry), never
   bare rectangles.
